# What, Why, and How

## 1. Why (Primary Goal)

What Alex really needs is to reduce friction in her orders, make the workflow faster, more reliable and scalable. Here are the [**pain-points 🎯**](https://github.com/manuyetilee/vitalChain-management/blob/main/documentation/pain_points.md).

**Secondary Goal:** Also would be nice to Improve internal organization and document consistency.

## 2. What

To solve her problems, an internal tool with the following capabilities is proposed **(Hypothesis)** :
- Enable workers have a friendly order information entry and without redundancy.
- Price calculation and visualization should be straight forward.
- Data entry redundancy should be reduced or eliminated. (ex, there is documentation that needs to be sent to third parties).
- Internal workflow consolidation(ex, approvals).
- Workers should be able to auto generate the documentation needed.
- Enable workers to access swiftly to the generated and organized documentation(audit trail).
- Company should have business rules around, like tracking product authorizations.

## 3. How

She presented different edge cases, therefore a web-based application is best fit for the solutions Alex needs and reduce time-to-market and maintenance overhead.
- Short timeframe from the conceive phase to the delivery phase therefore budget ceiling is low.
- Maintenance should be cheap.
- Fast iteration with user based data
- V1 needs to focus on UI and priority features , this means:
    - Replace excel workflow with structured intake + calculations.
    - Ensure pricing and billable amounts are correct and auditable.
    - Generate required documents in a consistent, repeatable way.
    - Minimize manual re-entry and reduce errors.
- Team is small one engineer and one Product Manager
    - Full Stack provides whats needed for web-based application.
    - UI/UX analyst replaced by the Product Manager.
- 2-week sprints deliverables
