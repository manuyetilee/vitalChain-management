# Alex Medical Supply: Key Pain Points

- Fragile spreadsheet: a single broken field can disrupt pricing and billing work.
- Complex pricing logic (vendor/product/state/payer) is hard to audit and easy to get wrong in Excel.
- Manual data re-entry into vendor portals wastes time and introduces inconsistencies.
- Approval for certain HCPCS codes happens outside the workflow, causing delays and context switching.
- Document generation (encounter form, invoice, POD) is manual and inconsistent across orders.
- Loose internal document organization and consistency makes it harder to find and trust records.
- Key business rules (for example, prior auth requirements) live in tribal knowledge, slowing training and scaling.
